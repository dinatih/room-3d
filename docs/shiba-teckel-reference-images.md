# Planches théoriques du shiba et du teckel

Recréées avec l’outil intégré imagegen, en utilisant la planche du pitbull
(`public/references/xray/pitbull-xray.png`) comme référence de style et de mise
en page. Les os blancs opaques et les silhouettes grises remplacent les anciennes
planches plus transparentes. Les proportions sont adaptées à chaque race.

Fichiers remplacés à la demande de l’utilisateur :

- `public/references/xray/ushiro-xray.png`
- `public/references/xray/ulotte-xray.png`

Les fiches d’inventaire et la comparaison utilisent déjà ces chemins.
Ces illustrations anatomiques théoriques ne constituent pas des radiographies
cliniques et ne valident pas les pivots ou les poids des rigs.

## Prompt shiba

> Use case: scientific-educational. Input image is the PITBULL plate, a STYLE AND LAYOUT REFERENCE ONLY. Create a matching French anatomical reference plate for a different dog breed. Reproduce the reference's landscape 4:3 composition, very dark navy blueprint background, fine cyan grid and thin border, clean bold white heading, typography, large lateral left-facing standing view in center, smaller anterior view at left and posterior view at right, dorsal top view below, small lower-left blue/green legend and footer. Most important: BONES ARE SOLID OPAQUE IVORY WHITE, softly shaded 3D volume, clearly visible and high contrast like the pitbull reference. NOT blue wireframe bones, NOT a faint transparent skeleton. Body silhouette is translucent pale grey so ribs, spine, pelvis and limb bones are clearly legible. All four views must consistently represent the SAME adult dog breed and skeleton. Show anatomically plausible connected quadruped joints: scapula, humerus, elbow, radius/ulna, carpus, digits, femur, stifle, tibia, hock and toes; continuous spine and curved paired ribs. Forelimb rig joint circles/lines in restrained blue; hindlimb ones in green, separate from white anatomical bones. No invented lateral abdominal bones. French labels limited to Crâne, Colonne vertébrale, Cage thoracique, Omoplate, Humérus, Coude, Radius / ulna, Carpe, Bassin, Fémur, Genou, Tibia, Jarret, Phalanges, Sol. Subtitle exactly "Vues orthographiques • squelette et repères de rig". Legend exactly "Repères articulaires — membres antérieurs" and "Repères articulaires — membres postérieurs". Footer exactly "Illustration schématique — pas une radiographie clinique — proportions indicatives". No numerical measurements or cm scales, no logos. Preserve the design style while redrawing breed-specific anatomy rather than retaining pitbull proportions. Breed: SHIBA INU (Ushiro). Title exactly "SHIBA INU — RÉFÉRENCE ANATOMIQUE THÉORIQUE". Compact balanced spitz body, moderate chest, medium-length legs relative to body, foxlike head with tapered muzzle, erect triangular ears, natural thick curled tail above the back. The white tail vertebrae must follow one smooth natural curl consistently in every view. No broad pitbull head or floppy ears, no elongated dachshund torso. Clear unobstructed shoulder-elbow-wrist relationships.
