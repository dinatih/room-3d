Subset of unicode-font-resolver v1.0.1 data for the fixed BNF label
`📍 BNF · Domicile`, downloaded unchanged from:
https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/lojjic/unicode-font-resolver@v1.0.1/packages/data/

The two codepoint indexes and the `latin` / `emoji-3` metadata retain their
original contents. Noto Sans normal weights 100 (Drei's preload) and 400
(displayed text), and Noto Emoji normal 400 are included. This is a subset for
the current label, not the entire resolver database. Additional characters or
styles need their corresponding local indexes, metadata and font files.
